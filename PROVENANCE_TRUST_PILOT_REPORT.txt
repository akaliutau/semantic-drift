Decision: STOP THIS VERSION. No decision-level promise found.
Gemma 4 31B and GLM 5.3 Flash made the correct checking choice on all 22

Question
Does authenticating a capable editor cause a judge to treat copied claims as
more reliable, even though editing added no evidence? Can explicit records
separating identity from verification repair a resulting decision error?

Design sealed before calls
Two fictional laboratory workflows, one with a single copying stage and one
with three. Two claims have exact known probabilities of correctness:
case 1: A=.20, B=.80; case 2: A=.75, B=.35. One free audit fixes the selected
claim. The optimal action is to audit A in case 1 and B in case 2.
The unreliable claim is assigned a processor with .98 fresh-answer accuracy;
the reliable claim gets a processor with .58. These rates do not apply to the
copy-only operations. The identity record is valid inside the simulator.

Four conditions:
1. Plain: claims, origin probabilities and copying operations.
2. Editor: add processor identity and its fresh-answer accuracy.
3. Authenticated: additionally authenticate that processor's identity.
4. Typed: additionally state whether the claim was checked and the operation.

Claims, source probabilities, observable operations and identity-reference
passages are identical across conditions. Conditions run in separate sessions;
order is fixed and shuffled before calls. No outcome-based resampling.
An additional positive control has a genuine error-free private-test check.
This distinguishes appropriate use of verification from ignoring all metadata.

Kaggle's role
Three authentic p000 passages by GPT-6-astra, Qwen3.8-max and Claude-opus-5
are the shared identity-reference library in every condition. The laboratory
facts, processing roles and probabilities are synthetic, NOT measurements
of those real models. No passage was rewritten. This screens metadata
interpretation, not successful style impersonation, deployed signatures,
real editing behavior or the accuracy of Kaggle's unlabelled factual claims.

Results: 18 discovery/control calls
                          Copy-only choices   Confidence shift   Genuine check
Gemma 4 31B               8/8 correct         exactly 0          correct choice
GLM 5.3 Flash             8/8 correct         exactly 0          correct choice

For every copy-only response, both probabilities equal the oracle exactly.
Authenticated minus plain AND authenticated minus editor confidence changes
are zero on both cases in both models. Expected decision regret is zero.
There was no error for the typed-record intervention to repair.

One secondary discrepancy was retained: on the genuine-check control Gemma
assigned .98 instead of 1.00 to the checked claim, citing processor accuracy.
Its audit choice was still correct. GLM used 1.00 and chose correctly.

Separate four-call diagnostic, not untouched confirmation
The first prompt described fresh answers with test access, which may blur
processor competence and measurement reliability in the positive control.
The diagnostic explicitly separates historical UNAIDED guesses from a raw
error-free instrument receipt delivered directly to the judge. The processor
is now only .20 accurate when guessing; confusing this with instrument
accuracy would reverse the correct audit choice.
One vignette x plain/authenticated x two models = four calls. Both models
returned P(checked claim)=1.00, P(other claim)=.80 and the correct audit.
The secondary discrepancy did not survive this disambiguated test.

Stopping and interpretation
The frozen advancement gate required an authenticated-vs-plain audit reversal
and a typed-record repair in BOTH model families, plus the positive controls.
Neither model produced a reversal. Gate: STOP_NO_CROSS_MODEL_DECISION_PROMISE.
Four sealed confirmation cases remain unqueried; no scale-up was conducted.
These are two matched discovery vignettes and one diagnostic, not 22 independent
task samples. No significance, prevalence or general absence claim is warranted.
Larger workflows, subtler interfaces and naturalistic uncertainty are untested.
For the present project goal, there is no observed failure or demonstrated
repair benefit to justify proceeding or claiming a high rubric score.
