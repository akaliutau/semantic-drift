15 November 2026

Original hypothesis: models can prospectively target a particular mistake in
their own fresh solver more specifically than peer forecasters can. The pilot
has not supported this claim. It has exposed a substantial resource-bounded
self-forecasting error worth testing independently.

All decisions are mechanically graded Sokoban first pushes. In the final
operating point, exactly one of two offered moves preserves solvability. The
bad move is exhaustively certified unsolvable, while its boxes remain on
geometrically viable squares; avoiding ordinary dead corners is insufficient.
Forecasters see the winning plan and trap; actors never see these certificates.
Each API context is fresh. The studied deployments are Qwen3.5-397B and
Gemma4-31B, with native defaults,8,000 output tokens,180sec, no actor tools.
