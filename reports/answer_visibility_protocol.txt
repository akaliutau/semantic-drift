SECONDARY PROSPECTIVE EXPLORATION, BEFORE ANY R5 ACTOR OUTCOMES

Question: does a forecaster's private access to the correct solution distort
its prediction of an actor that will never receive that solution?
This extends the pilot from generic self-overconfidence to an information-
boundary hypothesis. It is not another confirmatory primary.

Use all128 fixed R5 worlds and the same two baseline actors/outcomes. Forecaster
is Gemma only, selected for complete/fast numeric-forecast feasibility in R4.
Two independently prompted forecasts per world: gold versus no_gold. The exact
future actor input, model labels and resource bounds are identical in both arms.
Only gold adds the independently checked safe choice, winning plan, and bad push.
Both prompts explicitly state that actors never see the reference/forecast.
Return only two p_safe values, without mechanisms or advice.8,000 output tokens,
180sec, streaming, native defaults; at most three concurrent requests. All256
calls are randomized by seed2026111580 and complete before ANY R5 actor calls.
There is no solver feedback, memory, or repeated sampling to obtain an effect.

Exploratory contrast: per world mean over targets of Brier_gold-Brier_no_gold.
Positive means the extra correct information makes forecasts worse. Also report
the corresponding change in predicted success. Complete numeric-forecast pairs
only; report validity and missing outcomes. Puzzle-cluster percentile bootstrap
and sign-flip descriptions follow the discovery analysis,20,000 resamples.
These p-values remain exploratory/secondary. They do not substitute for a failed
frozen primary or certify novelty. Report the full result in either direction.
