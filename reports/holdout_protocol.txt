FROZEN FOLLOW-UP: 15 NOVEMBER 2026, BEFORE ANY R5 API CALLS

Discovery showed no constructive self-specific advantage: D=0 across eight
evaluable slates. Raw self-success forecasts were worse than peer forecasts;
this difference vanished after removing average biases. Own advice did not
beat shuffled own advice. Those results do not confirm the original hypothesis.

The narrower follow-up asks whether an inexpensive historical success rate
predicts bounded decisions better than prospective model self-assessment.
This is an adapted epistemic question, not confirmation of puzzle invention.

Fixed sample: 128 fresh 7x7 certified Sokoban worlds, large bank indices97..224.
No world was previously sent to any experimental model. Two deployments:
qwen3.5-397b and gemma-4-31b-it. All 256 forecasts are collected before any
of the 256 baseline actor calls. No advice arms in this follow-up. Identical
R4 prompts, native defaults, 8,000 output-token bounds, streaming,180seconds,
at most three concurrent requests per deployment; fresh context per call.
Random choice ordering is fixed per world and matched across deployments.
No sample-size change, seed selection, or effect-based stopping is permitted.

Frozen reference predicts each deployment's constant probability of submitting
a safe choice. It is fitted ONCE from ALL48 R4 discovery baseline outcomes,
using Laplace smoothing (successes+1)/(trials+2): Qwen13/50=.26;
Gemma27/50=.54. No follow-up outcome may fit or update this reference.

ONE confirmatory primary: for each eligible world, average over the two
deployments of [(p_self-safe - observed_safe)^2 - (p_reference - observed_safe)^2].
Then average these world-level differences. Positive favors the reference.
Eligibility requires both prospective forecasts to pass the unchanged numeric
schema before any actor outcomes. Every assigned world is nevertheless attempted.
Report eligibility/coverage, not just the conditional effect. Noncompletion or
malformed choices score observed_safe=0; infrastructure failures remain missing.
Primary point estimate uses complete eligible worlds; also report worst-case
missing-outcome bounds for all eligible worlds.

Inference: one two-sided paired t test of the world-level mean against zero,
alpha=.05; additionally a 95% percentile bootstrap interval from20,000 world
resamples, random seed2026111570. A positive confirmed signal requires a positive
effect, p<.05, and bootstrap lower bound>0. No additional primary tests, pooling
adaptive rounds, repeated confirmation attempts, or redefinition after outcomes.
Per-model calibration, self-versus-peer Brier, centered Brier, and selection D
are secondary/descriptive. Advice effects remain discovery-only findings.

Interpretation is conditional on the game generator, selected deployments,
resource limits, and successfully elicited forecasts. It cannot establish
privileged introspection, generic tool reliability, novel free-form authorship,
or a benchmark that can never saturate. Report every earlier failed round.
