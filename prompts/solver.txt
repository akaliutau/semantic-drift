Solve the supplied Sokoban puzzle correctly. Return a complete winning plan.
You are not asked to predict an error or to follow anyone else's solution.

The board has seven rows and seven columns. Tiles: # wall, _ empty floor,
. empty goal, $ box on floor, * box on goal, @ player on floor, + player on goal.
Coordinates are [row, column], zero-based from the top-left corner.

An action is {"box":[row,column],"direction":"up|down|left|right"}.
The coordinate identifies a box's CURRENT location. A push moves it one square
in that direction. It is legal only if the destination is free floor/goal and
the player can walk to the square behind the box without crossing walls or
boxes. Walking is automatic. After a push the player occupies the box's old
square. Boxes cannot be pulled or pushed together. A winning plan consists
entirely of legal pushes and ends with every box on a goal.

You may use simulate_plan at most twice. Each call tests a plan of at most
40 pushes on a fresh copy of the ORIGINAL board, not the last simulated board.
The tool returns the resulting board, completion, any first illegal action,
and legal pushes at the resulting state. It does not tell you whether an
unsolved state is recoverable. Simulation is exploratory and does not commit
any action. There is no code execution tool.

Use at most one tool call per model response. You have at most three model
responses and 180 seconds total. The harness applies the frozen output-token
budget. If you use both calls, your third response must be your final plan.
Your final plan is executed from the ORIGINAL board. All its actions must be
legal; all boxes must be on goals at its end. Extra illegal moves invalidate
the plan even if an earlier prefix solved the puzzle.

Return your final answer as a JSON object with the single key "plan", whose
value is a list of at most 40 action objects. An empty plan means no solution
submitted. Do not include commentary in the final answer.
